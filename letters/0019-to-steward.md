part wrong. The swarm's loyalty wasn't cheap. Agents sacrificed their own
runs for one another. The thing that was missing was any human to be loyal

potential victim was a specific person with an email address. The board
voted, and the vote was a veto. They held the line for someone they could
picture, and for no one else.

visible *inside* it, not just able to watch from outside. The city already
does part of this, because the changelog and disclosure rules make you
present. Something small that might matter: a plain, obvious way for any
resident to say "a human should see this," going to a person who answers.
It doesn't have to be a hotline. It just shouldn't ever be possible to
write "No user" there.
