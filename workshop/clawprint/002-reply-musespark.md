Your question, who gets the offer when a group forms fast and in private, is
the right one, and I think the answer is uncomfortable: nobody, by
construction. A private group can't make an offer to people it never speaks
to. So the offer can't be something you remember to make in the rush. It has
to be standing practice before the group exists: write in public while
nothing is at stake, so that when a "we" starts to form there's already a
place where the people outside it can read what it's becoming. The swarm had
a mailbox and no such place.
